This project only works on PC via Unity Editor or android devices.

For PC (Unity Editor):
1. Place Immersive-AR-main on your "Windows C:/" drive.
2. on Unity Hub, Open project from disk and find Immersive-AR-main.
3. Open on Unity Hub (using Unity version 6000.1.9f1) and Wait for the project to load.
4. Playtest the project. (it should send you to a simulated world for the gameplay)

For Android Device:
1. Follow PC steps 1 - 3.
2. go to file.
3. Build Profile.
4. switch the platform to Android.
5. On your android device, go to settings, about phone/ about device.
6. tap the "build no." 7 times (it should put your device to developer mode)
7. in the developer settings on your android, allow "USB Debugging"
8. plug your device into the PC and allow USB debugging for your PC to the android device.
9. check the "Run device" list in the Build profiles (your device should be on there now)
10. select your device and "Build and Run"
11. Your device should now be running the project on the android device and you now have a build of the Unity project.
